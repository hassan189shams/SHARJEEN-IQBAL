01 / The plan

A manifesto that
means something.

Keep every promise specific, realistic, and open to public follow-up. These are editable starter themes—not claims that any issue has already been resolved.

01
Academic support

Clearer academic communication

Raise recurring concerns about schedules, notices, examination updates and result delays through the appropriate university channels.

02
Student voice

Open, accessible representation

Make it easier for students across departments and halls to raise issues and receive a clear status update.

03
Campus facilities

Documented issue follow-up

Collect reports on classrooms, laboratories, libraries, Wi-Fi and shared facilities, then track referrals and responses.

04
Hostel life

Listen to hall-level concerns

Hold accessible conversations about maintenance, dining, water, cleanliness and other concerns students choose to raise.

05
Career readiness

More useful career information

Advocate for better visibility of internships, career events, skill-building opportunities and placement-related resources.

06
Welfare and inclusion

Respectful, fair support

Listen to student welfare concerns and direct them to the relevant official support services when appropriate.

07
Transparency

Updates students can check

Publish periodic summaries of issues raised, actions taken, responses received and matters still awaiting resolution.

08
Accountability

Promises with follow-through

Distinguish between what student representatives can do directly and what requires university approval or resources.
